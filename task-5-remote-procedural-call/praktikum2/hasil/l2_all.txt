Alasan: Protobuf mengenali field lewat NOMOR, bukan nama/urutan.

======================================================================
L2.4 — Ukuran Protobuf vs XML-RPC (payload, tanpa header)
======================================================================
| Pesan | Protobuf | XML-RPC | Rasio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Response Mahasiswa | 24 B | 350 B | 14.6x |
| Response + prodi   | 37 B | 350 B | 9.5x |
| Request satu NIM   | 9 B | 163 B | 18.1x |

Rincian Protobuf: nama field tidak dikirim (hanya nomor), angka biner.
gRPC menambah framing 5 B/pesan + HTTP/2 (HPACK); XML-RPC menambah
header HTTP/1.x teks. Rasio total tetap jauh lebih kecil di Protobuf.
