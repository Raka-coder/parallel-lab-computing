L1.2 — Marshalling XML-RPC & overhead format
======================================================================

--- methodCall tambah(5, 3) (190 byte) ---
<?xml version='1.0'?>
<methodCall>
<methodName>tambah</methodName>
<params>
<param>
<value><int>5</int></value>
</param>
<param>
<value><int>3</int></value>
</param>
</params>
</methodCall>


--- methodResponse (121 byte) ---
<?xml version='1.0'?>
<methodResponse>
<params>
<param>
<value><int>8</int></value>
</param>
</params>
</methodResponse>


| Pesan | Total | Data murni | Overhead | % overhead |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Request  | 190 B | 8 B | 182 B | 95.8 % |
| Response | 121 B | 4 B | 117 B | 96.7 % |
| Total    | 311 B | 12 B | 299 B | 96.1 % |

Catatan: header HTTP belum dihitung (biasanya 130-200 byte lagi per pesan).

--- Request statistik (list -> <array>) (389 byte) ---
<?xml version='1.0'?>
<methodCall>
<methodName>statistik</methodName>
<params>
<param>
<value><array><data>
<value><int>3</int></value>
<value><int>1</int></value>
<value><int>4</int></value>
<value><int>1</int></value>
<value><int>5</int></value>
<value><int>9</int></value>
<value><int>2</int></value>
<value><int>6</int></value>
</data></array></value>
</param>
</params>
</methodCall>


--- Response statistik (dict -> <struct>) (331 byte) ---
<?xml version='1.0'?>
<methodResponse>
<params>
<param>
<value><struct>
<member>
<name>min</name>
<value><int>1</int></value>
</member>
<member>
<name>max</name>
<value><int>9</int></value>
</member>
<member>
<name>rata2</name>
<value><double>3.875</double></value>
</member>
</struct></value>
</param>
</params>
</methodResponse>


Poin:
- list  -> <array><data><value>...</value></data></array>
- dict  -> <struct><member><name>..</name><value>..</value></member></struct>
- float -> <double>; kunci dict WAJIB string (spesifikasi XML-RPC)
- 8 angka memakai 389 byte; tiap elemen dibungkus beberapa tag,
  sehingga ukuran tumbuh jauh lebih cepat daripada data sebenarnya.
